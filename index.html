iFoundSound

Send a link, get the sound.

iFoundSound is a small private Discord bot for a few friends who make music. Paste a YouTube, SoundCloud or Instagram link in Discord and the bot sends back the audio at the best quality available, optionally trimmed to the part you want.

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the bot also saves each clip (the original file and/or WAV or AAC copies) into an iFoundSound folder in your own Drive, so it syncs to your laptop.

The bot can only see files it creates in your Drive. It cannot read anything else there.
